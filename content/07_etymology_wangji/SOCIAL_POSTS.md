## 📸 Instagram Reels
**Caption:**
Ever wonder why 忘记 (wàngjì) means "to forget" in Chinese? The etymology is beautifully literal! 🇨🇳

Let's break it down:
👉 **忘 (wàng) = Disappearing from the Heart**
It's made of 亡 (disappearing/lost) + 心 (heart). It means a thought has disappeared from your heart! 💔💨
👉 **记 (jì) = Record for Oneself**
It's made of 讠(speech/words) + 己 (oneself). It means using words to record something so you remember it yourself! 📝🐈

💡 **忘 + 记 = 忘记**
Letting what you recorded disappear from your heart = To forget! 🤯
