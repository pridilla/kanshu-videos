## 📸 Instagram Reels
**Caption:**
Why does 开始 (kāishǐ) mean "to start" in Chinese? 🚪🌱

Let’s break down the fascinating ancient visual story:

🚪 **开 (kāi)**: Originally depicted two hands sliding a wooden crossbar bolt away to unlatch a gate—lifting the barrier to open a clear path!
👩 **始 (shǐ)**: Combines **女 (nǚ)** (woman/mother) with **台 (tái)** (sound/origin). Ancient etymology traced the very origin of life back to a mother giving birth!

✨ **The Synthesis**:
**开** (Unlatching the gate) + **始** (Giving birth to new life) = **开始 (kāishǐ)**: Opening the way to bring brand-new life into the world—the ultimate beginning! 🚀
