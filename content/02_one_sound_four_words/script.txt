Okay so.

Chinese does something that just melts English speakers' brains.

Same sound. Four completely different words. The only thing that changes is your pitch. Your voice goes up, down, flat, sharp drop — and suddenly you're saying something else entirely.

The example everyone uses. The sound is ma.

First tone. Keep it high and flat. Like you're singing. ma.

Mother. Second tone. Start low, rise up. Like you're surprised. ma.

Hemp. Third tone. Dip down, come back up. Like you're really thinking about it. ma.

Horse. Fourth tone. Drop it hard. Like you're telling someone to stop. ma.

Scold.

Same sound. Four words.

Wrong pitch, wrong word. That's the whole thing.

Every learner gets caught by this. You can know the character cold and still mess it up. Just because your voice went up instead of down.

That's how Chinese tones work.
