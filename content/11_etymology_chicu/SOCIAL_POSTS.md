## 2. Instagram Reels
**Caption**:
Jealous in Chinese is 吃醋 (chīcù) — which literally translates to "EATING VINEGAR". But why?! 🍶🤯

Here is the ancient story behind this hilarious expression:

1️⃣ **Character 1: 吃 (chī)**
• Left: 口 (kǒu) — an open mouth
• Right: 乞 (qǐ) — a person begging for food
• Together: Swallowing food into your mouth!

2️⃣ **Character 2: 醋 (cù)**
• Left: 酉 (yǒu) — an ancient clay wine jar
• Right: 昔 (xī) — the sun setting over the past
• Together: Wine left in a jar for ages that ferments into sour vinegar!

👑 **The Legendary Origin**:
During the Tang Dynasty, Emperor Taizong wanted to reward his prime minister with a concubine, but his wife refused. The Emperor gave her a choice: accept the concubine, or drink this cup of deadly poison. Without a second thought, she gulped the entire cup! 

It was actually just vinegar. The Emperor was so touched by her stubborn devotion that he dropped the idea—and "eating vinegar" (吃醋) became the universal word for romantic jealousy!
