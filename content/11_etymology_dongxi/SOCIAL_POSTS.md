## 2. Instagram Reels 📸

### Caption:
Ever wondered why 东西 (dōngxi) means "things" or "stuff" in Chinese? 🧭✨

The literal translation is "East-West"! Here is the ancient story:

🌅 **东 (dōng) — East**: Originally depicted a travel sack tightly bundled onto a carrying pole, pointing towards the morning sunrise in the East.

🌇 **西 (xī) — West**: Originally showed a bird safely resting in its woven wicker nest at sunset in the West.

🛍️ **Synthesis — 东西 (dōngxi)**: In imperial capitals like Chang'an, commerce was strictly organized into the East Market (东市) and West Market (西市). When locals went shopping for supplies, they called it "buying East-West" (买东西)!

Over centuries, 东西 became the universal word for all physical items and objects in the universe.
