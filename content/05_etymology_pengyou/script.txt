Why does 朋友 (péngyǒu) feature two moons and two hands? Let's break it down! 朋 (péng) originally depicted two strings of valuable cowrie shells tied together, which later evolved into two side-by-side moons 月 (yuè). It symbolizes two equal companions bound by shared values. And 友 (yǒu)? It was originally drawn as two right hands extending in the same direction! It represents two friends reaching out to support each other. Put them together, and 朋友 (péngyǒu) literally means companions standing side-by-side, extending helping hands in true friendship!
