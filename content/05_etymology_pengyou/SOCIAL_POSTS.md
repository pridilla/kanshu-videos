Ancient Chinese characters are mind-blowing visual stories. Let's break down the secret history behind 朋友:

🔹 **朋 (péng) — Twin Companions**
In Oracle Bone script, 朋 depicted two strings of valuable cowrie shells tied side-by-side. Later simplified to two side-by-side moons (月 + 月), it symbolizes two equal companions bound together by shared values.

🔹 **友 (yǒu) — Helping Hands**
Look closely at 友: it was originally drawn as two right hands (𠂇 and 又) extending in the same direction! It depicts two friends reaching out paws to lift a heavy burden together.

✨ **Synthesis**:
朋 (side-by-side companions) + 友 (extending helping hands) = **朋友 (Friends who stand together and support each other!)**
