## 2. Instagram Reels

```text
Why does "Excuse me" in Chinese literally mean "Hitting Monkeys"?! 🐒🤯

Let us break down 打扰 (dǎrǎo):

1. 打 (dǎ) — Striking with Hands
• Left: 扌 (shǒu) — Hand radical
• Right: 丁 (dīng) — A nail being struck
• Meaning: Striking action with your hands! 🔨

2. 扰 (rǎo) — Swatting Chaotic Monkeys
• Left: 扌 (shǒu) — Another hand radical
• Right: 尤 (yóu) — A wild, troublesome beast (historically a mischievous monkey)
• Meaning: Agitating and swatting away wild monkeys! 🐒

3. The Synthesis: 打扰 (dǎrǎo)
When you combine striking with monkey chaos, you get "causing a disturbance". That is why when you politely interrupt someone, you say 打扰一下 (dǎrǎo yíxià) — literally "excuse my monkey chaos!"
```
