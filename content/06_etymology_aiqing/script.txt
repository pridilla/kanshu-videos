Why does 爱情 (àiqíng) contain a friend and a green heart? Let's break down 爱 (ài) first. The top part 爫 (zhǎo) represents a hand reaching down, and the bottom part 友 (yǒu) represents a friend. Together, 爱 (ài) means reaching out to embrace a friend with deep affection!
Now let's look at 情 (qíng). On the left, 忄 (xīn) represents the heart and our emotions. On the right, 青 (qīng) represents the color green, symbolizing youth and vitality. Together, 情 (qíng) means the blossoming, youthful emotions of the heart!
Put them together, and 爱情 (àiqíng) literally means the deep, blossoming affection between two people in love!
