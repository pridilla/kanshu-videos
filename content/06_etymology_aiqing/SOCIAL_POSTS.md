Why does 爱情 (àiqíng) mean LOVE in Chinese? Let's look at the character origins! 🇨🇳

❤️ 爱 (ài): The top 爫 represents a hand reaching down, and the bottom 友 represents a friend. Reaching out to embrace a friend with deep affection!
🌿 情 (qíng): The left 忄 is the heart. The right 青 means green or youth. The blossoming, youthful emotions of the heart!

Combined, 爱情 is the deep, blossoming affection between two people! 🥰

Ever wondered why the Chinese word for love has a friend and a green heart in it? Let's break it down!

✨ **爱 (ài)**: Contains a hand (爫) reaching out to a friend (友). It represents embracing someone with deep affection!
✨ **情 (qíng)**: Contains the heart (忄) and the color green (青), symbolizing youth and vitality. The youthful, blossoming emotions of the heart!

Put them together, and 爱情 is the beautiful, blossoming affection between two people! 🥰
