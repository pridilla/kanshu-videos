## Research Summary — Platform Best Practices (2025-2026)

### YouTube Shorts
- **Title:** 60-70 characters max. Front-load the keyword. Use numbers, parentheses, or power words to increase CTR. Avoid clickbait.
- **Description:** First 2 lines (≈100 chars) are above the fold and appear in search — make them count. Include timestamps if multi-scene, 2-3 relevant hashtags at the bottom, and 1-2 links (website, related video).
- **Tags:** 8-12 tags. Mix of broad ("learn Chinese"), specific ("Krashen silent period"), and long-tail ("how to start learning Mandarin"). Order matters less than it used to, but still helps for disambiguation.
- **Thumbnail:** Not shown in Shorts feed, but appears on channel page and search — still worth making. High contrast, max 3 words, face/emoji if possible.
- **Best posting:** 2-4 PM local time weekdays; 9-11 AM weekends.

### Instagram Reels
- **Caption:** 125-150 characters is the sweet spot for reach. The first line is the hook. Use line breaks for readability.
- **Hashtags:** 3-5 hashtags is the current best practice (the "30 hashtags" era is dead; Instagram penalizes hashtag spam). Mix: 1 broad, 2-3 niche, 1 branded.
- **Keywords in caption:** Instagram SEO now indexes caption text for search — include target keywords naturally.
- **CTA:** Ask a question or prompt a save/share. Saves and shares now weight more than likes in the algorithm.
- **Alt text:** Add for accessibility + algorithmic understanding.
- **Best posting:** 11 AM - 1 PM & 7 PM - 9 PM local time.
- **Cover frame:** Upload a custom cover (1080×1920) with readable title text — crucial for profile grid aesthetics.

### TikTok
- **Caption:** Under 100 characters typically performs best. Hook in the first 3-5 words. Questions and statements both work.
- **Hashtags:** 3-4 max. 1 trending (if relevant), 1-2 niche, 1 branded.
- **Keywords:** TikTok's search algorithm heavily weights caption text and on-screen text. Include primary keyword in both.
- **CTA:** "Follow for more language myths" or similar works well. Comments drive distribution.
- **Best posting:** 7 PM - 11 PM local time. Tuesday, Thursday, Friday highest engagement days.
- **Cover:** Custom cover recommended (1080×1920).
