Chinese is wild. To say I like you, you literally combine beating a victory war drum with cheering until you're breathless! Let's break down 喜 (xǐ) first. Originally depicting a celebratory drum 壴 (zhù) over a singing mouth 口 (kǒu), 喜 (xǐ) represents the explosive joy of celebrating a grand triumph! Now look at 欢 (huān). On the left, a bird sings, while on the right, 欠 (qiàn) shows a person with their mouth wide open, cheering and gasping with excitement! Put them together, and 喜欢 (xǐhuan) literally means celebrating a victory with joyful cheers—which is the ultimate ancient definition of falling in love with someone!
