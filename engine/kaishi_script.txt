Why does 开始 (kāishǐ) contain opening a gate and a woman giving birth? Let's break down 开 (kāi) first. Originally depicting hands sliding a wooden bolt away to open a gate, 开 (kāi) represents lifting the barrier to clear a brand new path! Now let's look at 始 (shǐ). On the left, 女 (nǚ) represents a woman or mother. On the right, 台 (tái) provides the sound. Together, 始 (shǐ) originally meant the very beginning of human life through birth! Put them together, and 开始 (kāishǐ) literally means unlatching the gate to bring new life into the world—which is the ultimate way to start something new!
