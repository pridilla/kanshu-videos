Chinese is wild. To say "thing" or "stuff", you literally say "East-West"! But why? Let's break down 东 (dōng) first. Originally depicting a travel sack bundled tightly onto a carrying pole, 东 (dōng) points toward the sunrise in the East! Now look at 西 (xī). It originally showed a bird perching safely in its woven nest at sunset in the West. In ancient capitals like Chang'an, shopping was only allowed in the East and West imperial markets—so buying goods was literally "buying East and West"! Put them together, and 东西 (dōngxi) became the ultimate word for all physical things!
