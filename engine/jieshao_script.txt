Why does 介绍 (jièshào) contain a person between boundaries and a silk thread? Let's break down 介 (jiè) first. The outer strokes 八 (bā) represent boundaries, while the central part 人 (rén) represents a person. Together, 介 (jiè) originally meant a person standing between two things, acting as a go-between! Now let's look at 绍 (shào). On the left, 纟 (sī) represents a continuous silk thread. On the right, 召 (zhào) provides the sound. Together, 绍 (shào) means to link or connect things together like an unbroken thread! Put them together, and 介绍 (jièshào) literally means acting as a go-between to link two parties together—which is exactly what you do when you introduce someone!
